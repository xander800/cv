Modernising a 100-billion-record enterprise warehouse

Query execution cut from ~60 min to 2–3 min · 30% less manual migration effort
AT&T · May 2024 – Jul 2026

Problem

50+ enterprise applications running on Vertica, Oracle/GoldenGate and Mainframe needed to move to Snowflake without breaking downstream business logic or production stability.

Scale

~100 billion historical records. 50+ IBM DataStage procedures modernised into Azure Databricks notebooks.

Approach

Redesigned joins and execution strategies; built an internal Oracle-to-SnowSQL conversion tool; automated Snowflake-compatible DDL generation and validation.

Outcome

Query execution cut from ~60 min to 2–3 min. Manual migration effort per procedure reduced by 30%. Copilot-based engineering framework lifted developer productivity by up to 60%.

How I work

Principles that show up across every migration

→

Understand the source system's business logic before transforming it — dependency analysis comes before design.

→

Validate data at every migration stage, not just at the end — AP/AR datasets exceeding 500M records don't get a second chance.

→

Automate repetitive conversion work — BTEQ-to-Snowflake and Oracle-to-SnowSQL tooling turned manual effort into reusable pipelines.

→

Optimise based on evidence — query redesigns are driven by execution plans, not guesses.

→

Build for production, not demos — phased cutover planning with Product Owners and Business Analysts, not a one-shot switch.

→

Use AI tooling with guardrails, not blind trust — skill-routing frameworks are impact-scored and reviewed before merging.
